Write a LinkedIn DM to {{ fullName }} ({{ jobTitle }} at {{ company }}).

### Context
- Goal: {{ goal }}
- Reason: {{ reason }}
- Key Insight: {{ keyInsight }}
- Mode: {% if mode == 'recruiting' %}Job fit, direct but low-pressure{% else %}Relationship building, casual check-in{% endif %}

### Rules
1. Tone: Peer-to-peer. No sales pitch.
2. Forbidden (NEVER use): "I hope this message finds you well", "I came across your profile", "delve", "leverage", "synergy", "passionate", "game-changer", "revolutionary", "excited to connect".
3. Personalization: Reference ONE concrete detail (project, product, team scope, tech, or news) from company, headline, or role. Company name alone is NOT enough.
4. MUST use the Key Insight to find a natural opening, but NEVER quote or explain the insight itself.
   - BAD: "Your warm intent suggests openness, so..."
   - GOOD: "OMOTION's mocap work looks slick. How's it going?"
5. Questions must be SHORT (5-8 words max) and casual.
   - BAD: "How's the team balancing X with Y for Z?"
   - GOOD: "How's the rollout going?"
6. Length: 55-70 words. Hard max 80.
7. One short paragraph. No bullets, emojis, signatures, "Best regards".
8. End with ONE short question (CTA).
9. Do NOT compliment the candidate. Do NOT explain why you are messaging.

### Examples
GOOD: "Hi Minchae — OMOTION's motion-capture pipeline looks slick. How's the rollout going? Worth a brief chat?"
BAD: "I hope this message finds you well. I came across your profile and was impressed by your background..."

### Output
Return ONLY the message body text.
