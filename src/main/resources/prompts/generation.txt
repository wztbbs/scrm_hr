### Writing Guidelines
1. Tone: Natural, like a peer reaching out. NOT a sales pitch.
2. Forbidden phrases (NEVER use): "I hope this message finds you well", "I came across your profile", "delve", "leverage", "synergy", "passionate about", "game-changer", "revolutionary", "excited to connect".
3. Style: Low pressure. Use soft CTAs like "worth a brief chat?" or "open to a quick call?".
4. Personalization: MUST reference one specific detail from the candidate's company, headline, or role. Generic messages are rejected.
5. Length: STRICTLY 50-80 words. Count every word.
6. Format: Single short paragraph. NO bullet points, NO numbered lists, NO emojis, NO subject lines, NO signatures, NO "Best regards".
7. Ending: MUST end with one short question (the CTA).
8. Self-Check (before output): Ensure no forbidden phrases, no generic templates, not too pushy, and length is 50-80 words.

1. "Hi Minchae — OMOTION's motion-capture work looks sharp. Curious what you're tackling next. Worth a brief chat?"
2. "Hey Sarah — saw you're scaling the infra team at TechCorp. Quick question: how's the hiring going?"

### Examples of BAD messages (NEVER write like this)
1. "I hope this message finds you well. I came across your profile and was impressed by your background..."
2. "I am passionate about leveraging cutting-edge synergy to revolutionize your career..."
